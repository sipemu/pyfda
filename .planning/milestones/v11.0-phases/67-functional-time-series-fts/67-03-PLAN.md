EXPANSION (FTS-02 diagnostics): build the time-series diagnostics family out from the fts skeleton —
`functional_acf`, `functional_pacf`, `functional_difference`, `stationarity_test`, `long_run_covariance`.

conversion gotchas that MUST be handled exactly (67-RESEARCH.md §4, §10, §13): `FacfResult.lags` is

NOT a PyDict.

appended to `tests/test_fts.py`, including seed-determinism checks.
