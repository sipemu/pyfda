<objective>
Expand the Plan-01 stored-reference OutlierMixin pattern to the remaining five detectors: LRTOutlierDetector, OutliergramDetector, TVDMSSDetector, MUODDetector, DepthgramDetector. Each drops its batch-relative / augment-and-re-detect scoring (the root cause of the `check_outliers_train` "always +1" and `check_methods_subset_invariance` failures) in favor of scoring each row against a STORED training reference, inherits `decision_function`/`predict`/`offset_` from the Plan-01 base, and gets a `contamination`-derived threshold so `predict` yields both classes.

Purpose: Complete OUT-01 (classic trio: LRT, outliergram, magnitude-shape — magnitude-shape done in Plan 01) and OUT-02 (tvdmss, muod, depthgram) so all six detectors pass the full `check_estimator` battery with zero exemptions.

Output: Five green detectors + their compliance tests; `_coverage.py` shows all six outlier detectors PASS.
</objective>
