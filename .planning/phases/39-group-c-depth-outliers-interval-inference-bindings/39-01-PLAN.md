<objective>
Extend the `fdars.depth` functional-depth dispatcher (`depth_method_from_str` in `src/depth_mod.rs`) with the 9 new parameter-free `DepthMethod` variants shipped in fdars-core 0.23.0, so both `functional_depth(method=...)` and `functional_boxplot(method=...)` accept 13 methods total (per D — CONTEXT.md locked decision DEPTH-03). This is the smallest, most self-contained of the three Group C plans: a single dispatcher match + error-message rewrite + test coverage, touching only `src/depth_mod.rs` and `tests/test_depth.py`.

Purpose: Deliver DEPTH-03 — the 13-variant depth dispatcher with an accurate wildcard error message, no signature change, and full pytest coverage of all 9 new tokens plus the invalid-method path.
Output: An extended `depth_method_from_str` (9 new arms + rewritten wildcard message) in `src/depth_mod.rs`, updated docstrings on `functional_depth` / `functional_boxplot`, and a `TestFunctionalDepthNewVariants` + `TestFunctionalBoxplotNewMethods` + extended invalid-method coverage in `tests/test_depth.py`.
</objective>
