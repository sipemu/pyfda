<objective>
Expand the stored-model-predict pattern proven in Plan 01 across the differentiator regressors and the full FPC classifier family, promoting each to `check_estimator`-green under a per-estimator `parametrize_with_checks` battery. Delivers REG-02, CLF-01, CLF-02.

Purpose: the native classification functions (`fclassif_lda/qda/knn/dd`, `elastic_multinomial`) are TRANSDUCTIVE — they fit+predict on the whole batch, expose only `predicted`/`accuracy` (no reusable per-class model, no separate predict entrypoint). The Phase-55 `_BaseFdarsClassifier.predict` vstacks train+test then slices, which contaminates train with test and breaks subset-invariance (always predicts class 0 on a subset). This plan replaces that with a genuine reconstructed predict: FPC projection (reusing the exact FPCA the FPCATransformer uses) + a per-point discriminant/vote computed from stored fit state. RobustFPCRegressor uses the native `predict_fregre_robust` (has a real predict entrypoint); GLM/Nonparametric reconstruct in numpy from stored fit state.
Output: 3 differentiator regressors + 6 classifiers green; their `_coverage.py` verdicts flipped to PASS; compliance test files extended/created.
</objective>
