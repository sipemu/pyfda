fdars is a broad, method-accurate, Rust-accelerated Python library for
functional data analysis, providing \nsubmodules{} submodules spanning
representation, smoothing, depth, FPCA, alignment, clustering, classification,
regression, functional time series, and monitoring, with full scikit-learn
compatibility (\nsklearnestimators{} estimators passing \texttt{check\_estimator})
and a grounded AI advisor backed by a machine-readable scientific-provenance layer.
